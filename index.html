24K vs 22K Gold

24K gold has the highest purity, while 22K gold is commonly used for jewellery. Jewellery prices can be higher than the displayed gold rate because of GST, making charges and jeweller premiums.

HOW IT WORKS

How Gold Rates Are Calculated

GoldRate India displays the latest available reference gold price and calculates indicative 22K and 18K rates from the 24K reference purity. Your final jewellery bill can differ because of taxes, making charges and local premiums.

FAQ

Gold Rate FAQs

Is this the final jewellery price?

No. Jewellery stores may add GST, making charges and other applicable premiums.

Why can two cities have different prices?

Local premiums, logistics, taxes and jeweller pricing can cause differences.
